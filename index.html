About Crayon Rush

Before everything had to look perfect.

Crayon Rush is built around opening a fresh notebook, grabbing whatever color was closest and drawing a whole universe without asking permission.

The characters are intentionally rough, simple and full of motion. They are meant to feel like something you might have drawn after school, a wolf running too fast, a ghost wearing a cape, a red creature racing through the page or a strange hero digging into another imaginary world.
